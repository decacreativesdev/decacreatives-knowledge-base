# Indusolutions quote pricing

**Decision:** quote the [Indusolutions website](../projects/indusolutions-website.md) now, before the [open questions](2026-10-28-indusolutions-quote-open-questions.md) are answered, as a fixed price on written assumptions. Decided by [Joshua Clifford Reyes](../people/joshua-clifford-reyes.md) on 2026-10-28. The draft is **under the operator's review** and not sent. [pricing note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-indusolutions-quote-pricing.md)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Rate | USD 20 per hour ([rate note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-hourly-rate.md)) |
| Base build, 21 line items, English only | 83 hours + 10% contingency = 91.3 hours = USD 1,826 fixed |
| Optional: conditional or multi-step form, extra service page, extra content page, cookie banner, redirects, basic SEO, extra round, live training | USD 60, 20 each, 10 each, 40, 40, 50, 50, 30 |
| WP Care | USD 40 per site per month, up to 2 hours included, extra at USD 20/h, no email support - same as [Micheline](2026-10-28-micheline-quote-pricing.md), [Khoury](2026-10-28-khoury-quote-pricing.md) and [AIP](2026-10-28-aip-quote-pricing.md) |

**Why:**

- **Quote now:** Camille needs the number; it was wanted for her 2026-10-26 budget meeting. Each open question became a written assumption (up to 3 service pages and 5 content pages, a one-step quote form of up to 12 fields, SiteGround, 3 rounds, Figma with mobile before the build) and anything that could swing the price became an add-on.
- **Same layout as Khoury and Micheline:** fixed price plus contingency, so Camille sees one format across the quotes.
- **Scope:** the current scope from the [clarifications excerpt](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-indusolutions-clarifications-answers.md), not the [brief](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-indusolutions-quote-brief.md)'s; the video hero and the migration are not priced.
- **Timeline:** about three and a half weeks from the Figma handover. Launch by about 2026-12-04 needs the Figma by about 2026-11-09; the Figma has no date yet.
- **Hours:** Claude Code's estimate from the brief and clarifications only - there is no design. To be checked by the operator.

**Line items and assumptions:** the repo's [quote](../../../repos/indusolutions/docs/quote.md) and [quote.csv](../../../repos/indusolutions/docs/quote.csv); [quote-v2.csv](../../../repos/indusolutions/docs/quote-v2.csv) is the same in Epic, Story, Hours with no totals.
