# AIP quote v2

**Decision:** the [Auto Industrial Paints website](../projects/auto-industrial-paints-website.md) quote is **USD 1,815 fixed** for the base build, in the same layout as the Indusolutions, Khoury and Micheline quotes. Accepted by [Joshua Clifford Reyes](../people/joshua-clifford-reyes.md) on 2026-10-28. [v2 note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-aip-quote-v2.md)

**Supersedes:** the core price in [AIP quote pricing](2026-10-28-aip-quote-pricing.md) (USD 1,120-1,660 range). The rate (USD 20/h), WP Care (USD 40/site/month) and the reasoning on that page still stand.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Subtotal | 82.5 hours = USD 1,650 |
| Contingency (10%) | 8.25 hours = USD 165 |
| **Base build, fixed** | **90.75 hours = USD 1,815** |
| Add-ons | form, mega menu, cookie banner, formal accessibility audit, extra round, live training (USD 30-160 each) |
| WP Care | USD 40 per site per month, up to 2 hours included |

**Why higher than the v1 range:** single figures near the middle of each v1 range; four lines the other quotes carry (kickoff, privacy and 404, accessibility basics, project management); 10% contingency like the others; +1 hour for a menu down to article level, as the [brief](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-24-aip-quote-brief.md) asks.

**Where it follows the brief rather than the other quotes:** no SEO add-on and no SEO plugin, since the brief rules out SEO setup; no licence cost, since Elementor Pro is already held; the build kept open to a WooCommerce shop in phase two.

**Detail:** the repo's [quote](../../../repos/auto-industrial-paints/docs/quote.md) has the line items' reasoning and a table checking v2 against each point in the brief; `quote-v2.csv` beside it has the line items.
