# Khoury quote pricing

**Decision:** quote the [Khoury Construction website](../projects/khoury-construction-website.md) now, before the [open questions](2026-10-28-khoury-quote-open-questions.md) are answered, as a fixed price on written assumptions. Decided by [Joshua Clifford Reyes](../people/joshua-clifford-reyes.md) on 2026-10-28. The draft is **under the operator's review** and not sent. [pricing note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-khoury-quote-pricing.md)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Rate | USD 20 per hour ([rate note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-hourly-rate.md)) |
| Base build, 25 line items | 115 hours + 10% contingency = 126.5 hours = USD 2,530 fixed |
| Optional: WPML French + English, hero video, history timeline, basic SEO, extra round | USD 240, 60, 100, 60, 50 |
| Optional: extra migration | USD 6 per project beyond 30, USD 4 per post beyond 20 |
| WP Care | USD 40 per site per month, up to 2 hours included, extra at USD 20/h, no email support - same as [AIP quote pricing](2026-10-28-aip-quote-pricing.md) |

**Why:**

- **Quote now:** Camille needs the cost to pitch. The [brief](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-khoury-quote-brief.md) leaves the sitemap "To confirm", so each open question became a written assumption (pages, up to 30 projects, up to 20 posts, French only, SiteGround, 3 rounds) and anything that could swing the price became an add-on.
- **Fixed price, unlike AIP's range:** the contingency absorbs small changes once the answers arrive; bigger ones are quoted at the add-on rates. The two quotes do not yet share a layout.
- **Hours:** Claude Code's estimate from the brief only - there is no design. To be checked by the operator, and again once the Figma arrives.
- **WP Care USD 40:** set by the operator to match AIP, so Camille sees one price across her clients.
- **Not from the Khoury brief:** WP Care, Search Console and conversion tracking, and the basic SEO add-on come from the clarifications doc; the brief calls SEO "a separate service" after launch. Kept for now pending review.

**Line items and assumptions:** the repo's [quote](../../../repos/khoury-construction/docs/quote.md) and `quote.csv` beside it.
