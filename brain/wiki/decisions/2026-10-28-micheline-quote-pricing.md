# Micheline quote pricing

**Decision:** quote the [Micheline Paquette website](../projects/micheline-paquette-website.md) now, before the [open questions](2026-10-28-micheline-quote-open-questions.md) are answered, as a fixed price on written assumptions. Decided by [Joshua Clifford Reyes](../people/joshua-clifford-reyes.md) on 2026-10-28. The draft is **under the operator's review** and not sent. [pricing note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-micheline-quote-pricing.md)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Rate | USD 20 per hour ([rate note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-hourly-rate.md)) |
| Base build, 21 line items, EN and FR | 69 hours + 10% contingency = 75.9 hours = USD 1,518 fixed |
| Optional: a page per project, gallery grouped by medium, basic SEO, extra round | USD 120, 40, 40, 40 |
| WP Care | USD 40 per site per month, up to 2 hours included, extra at USD 20/h, no email support - same as [Khoury](2026-10-28-khoury-quote-pricing.md) and [AIP](2026-10-28-aip-quote-pricing.md) |

**Why:**

- **Quote now:** Camille needs the number. Each open question became a written assumption (projects as a home page section, up to 40 gallery images, SiteGround, 3 rounds, Figma with mobile before the build) and anything that could swing the price became an add-on.
- **Same layout as Khoury:** fixed price plus contingency, so Camille sees one format across the quotes.
- **Scope:** the simplified portfolio from the [clarifications excerpt](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-micheline-clarifications-answers.md), not the [brief](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-micheline-quote-brief.md)'s; light and dark mode is not priced. Bilingual EN and FR is in the base because both sources confirm it.
- **Timeline:** about three weeks from the Figma handover. End of December needs the Figma by about 2026-12-04; mid January by about 2026-12-18. Camille's Figma date is "ideally end of Dec".
- **Hours:** Claude Code's estimate from the brief and clarifications only - there is no design. To be checked by the operator.

**Line items and assumptions:** the repo's [quote](../../../repos/micheline-paquette/docs/quote.md) and `quote.csv` beside it; `quote-v2.csv` is the same quote as Epic, Story, Hours with no totals (added 2026-10-28, same price).
