Madz sent Camille two quotes, two extras and a build approach by email on 2026-11-27, through Google Drive links. All at USD 20/h. [detail note](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-27-note-madz-haskour-quotes-detail.md)

| Sheet | Hours | USD |
|---|---|---|
| Website | 77 | 1,540 |
| Intake form and automation | 47 | 940 |
| Extra, if the client wants it: clients log in instead of entering their email first | 15 | 300 |
| Extra, if the client wants it: Azure hosting instead of SiteGround | 14 | 280, plus Azure's own monthly cost |

- **Automation is a fixed price.** USD 940 covers one intake form of any length, with branching. It does not wait on the client's questionnaire: "our estimates/pricing must cover how simple or complicated it is" (Madz).
- **The proposal in the build approach:** WordPress and Elementor Pro on SiteGround, built from Camille's Figma. The client enters their email first, answers the Microsoft Form, then gets an emailed link to upload slips to their own SharePoint folder (one folder per client, one per tax year). Power Automate files the answers and notifies the firm. Microsoft Forms cannot take uploads from people outside the firm's own Microsoft 365, which is why the upload goes through a link.
- **Not dictated:** pages, sitemap and sections follow Camille's Figma design.
