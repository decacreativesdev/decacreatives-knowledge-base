# Auto Industrial Paints website

Client website for [Auto Industrial Paints](../external/auto-industrial-paints.md), brought in by [Camille Malloch](../people/camille-malloch.md). Studio-side owner: [Joshua Clifford Reyes](../people/joshua-clifford-reyes.md) (assumed - see `STATE.md` -> "Active Decisions"). Stack: SiteGround + WordPress + Elementor Pro. Code: [Auto Industrial Paints repo](../systems/auto-industrial-paints.md).

- **Status:** quoting, not started. No deadline stated. Client status unconfirmed (operator: "I think so").
- **What it is:** a new guides and content hub on car paint, yacht coatings and protective coatings for `autoindustrialpaints.com` (no existing site), pointing DIY owners and trade buyers to suppliers. About 5 template types: home, category, topic hub, article, plus Where to buy and About as home sections. The client edits articles and categories. No backend; site search and recent/trending guides only. [quote brief](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-24-aip-quote-brief.md)
- **Commercial:** 3 review rounds, feedback only from Camille. Launch "the sooner the better once the quote is accepted". Quote wanted as soon as possible, with a separate monthly WP Care price. Out of scope: e-commerce (phase two, WooCommerce), SEO, email support, copywriting. [quote brief](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-24-aip-quote-brief.md)
- **Design:** the HTML mockups in `docs-local/html-design-files/` are a reference sample only. The final design will be Figma, not yet provided - see [decision](../decisions/2026-10-28-aip-html-mockups-reference-only.md). This supersedes the brief's "please build from these". [operator note](../../sources/2026/10/2026-10-28-note-aip-repo-setup.md)
- **Quote:** USD 1,815 fixed for the base build, WP Care USD 40 per site per month, in the same layout as the other three quotes and checked against the brief. Not sent yet. See [quote v2](../decisions/2026-10-28-aip-quote-v2.md).
