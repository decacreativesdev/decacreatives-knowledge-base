# Haskour CPA website

New client [Haskour CPA](../external/haskour-cpa.md), brought in by [Camille Malloch](../people/camille-malloch.md) on 2026-11-25. Studio-side builder: [Madz](../people/madz.md). Madz set up the repo `repos/haskour-cpa` ([haskour-cpa](../systems/haskour-cpa.md)) and sent Camille the quotes by email on 2026-11-27: website USD 1,540, automation USD 940, extras login USD 300 and Azure USD 280. See [quotes sent](../decisions/2026-11-27-haskour-cpa-quotes-sent.md). _(Updated 2026-11-27: this page said not quoted yet, then that the amounts were not recorded.)_ [detail note](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-27-note-madz-haskour-quotes-detail.md) [sent note](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-27-note-madz-haskour-quotes-sent.md) [overview](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-25-haskour-cpa-project-overview.md) [WhatsApp](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-25-whatsapp-camille-madz-haskour-deadlines.md)

- **Goal:** brand refresh, premium website and a Microsoft-based client intake form, all live before tax season opens on 2027-03-01.
- **Timeline:** kickoff 2026-12-07; Camille finishes designs by end of Dec 2026; hard deadline 2027-02-15, built, tested and live. Madz builds at their own pace.
- **Website:** premium but simple, layout modelled on mnp.ca. The client portal stays on the firm's external tax software; the site links out to it.
- **Intake and automation:** one Microsoft Form following the Canadian personal tax questionnaire, with document upload, behind an email gate. Completed forms and tax slips should file themselves into SharePoint. Priced separately from the website.
- **Brand refresh:** new CPA name, due in about 2 weeks from 2026-11-25 (to confirm with the client), and a slight logo refresh. Camille's side.
- **Marketing:** Green Horizon 10-video package, a monthly newsletter article with 5 social posts, and a January office photo shoot; campaign on Instagram and LinkedIn in the last 2 weeks of Feb 2027. Camille's side.
- **Two quotes wanted from Madz,** website and automation, by Monday morning 2026-11-30. Madz replied on 2026-11-25 aiming for "EOD tomorrow"; sent by 2026-11-27 ([sent note](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-27-note-madz-haskour-quotes-sent.md)).
- **Camille's questions for Madz:** can Microsoft Forms take file uploads natively, or does it need custom code; would the site be hosted on Microsoft Azure with WordPress and Elementor Pro. Answered in the build approach sent 2026-11-27: Forms cannot take uploads from people outside the firm, so clients get an emailed upload link to SharePoint, set up with Power Automate; SiteGround instead of Azure, with Azure as an extra. [detail note](../../sources/2026/11/2026-11-27-note-madz-haskour-quotes-detail.md)
